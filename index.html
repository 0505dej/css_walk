Grocery Shopping

I ran out of salt so I stopped by Whole Foods.
I grabbed a green basket and walked to the produce section.

A shiny red apple stared at me and said

"I'm delicious!"

so I grabbed it and placed it in my green basket.

Then I thought some peanut butter would go well with my apple.
I walked to the jam corner.

Amongst blueberry jam, grape jam, and strawberry jam, I found peanut butter!

I placed it in my green basket next to my red apple.

Came back home with my shiny red apple and peanut butter
and realized...

I forgot my salt!
